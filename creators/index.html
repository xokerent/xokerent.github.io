The game in 15 seconds

The calm number puzzle where every combo is yours

Totix: Number Match Puzzle is free on iPhone and iPad. Drag numbered tiles onto a 4×4 board and line up three or four of the same color, the same number, or a run like 3-4-5 for a Triple or a Quad. There’s no gravity, so tiles stay where you put them and every combo is planned. Match on back-to-back moves and the chain multiplier climbs: ×1.5, ×2.5, ×4, ×6.

Talking points (your words; use any or none)

  • No gravity, so every combo is one you planned.
  • Five modes: Training Arena, Treasure Hunt (50 hand-built levels, including fog and boss levels), Zen, Daily Event and Weekly League.
  • No lives and no waiting: retry any level.
  • Plays offline.
  • Every color also has its own shape (Settings → Color-blind shapes).

The essentials

Must-dos and please-don’ts

Must do

  • Disclose. Put “#ad” or “Sponsored” on screen in the first few seconds and say it out loud. Also switch on the platform label: TikTok Content disclosure → Branded content, Instagram Paid partnership, or YouTube Includes paid promotion. This applies to free in-app items and codes too.
  • Link your personal App Store link in your bio, caption or description. It counts your installs, and we’ll share the numbers with you.
  • Tag @totixgame.
  • Post on your agreed date, between Oct 26 and Nov 8, 2026.

Please don’t

  • Say “no ads”, “ad-free” or “no timer” (the Daily Event and Weekly League have a move clock).
  • Say tiles fall or cascade, claim health or brain benefits, or mention “#1”, awards, ratings or download numbers.
  • Ask viewers to rate or review the app.
  • Hide the disclosure, or use sounds that aren’t cleared for paid posts. Use TikTok’s Commercial Music Library or original audio.
