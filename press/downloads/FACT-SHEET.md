## One line

Totix is a cozy underwater number puzzle for iPhone and iPad: place tiles on a 4×4 board, match colors, numbers or runs, and chain combos on back-to-back moves.

## Short description

Totix: Number Match Puzzle is a free puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Players drag numbered tiles onto a 4×4 board and complete lines of three (a Triple) or four (a Quad) that share a color, share a number, or count in a run such as 3-4-5. Tiles never fall, so every combo is planned, and matching on consecutive moves builds a chain multiplier. Version 1.1 adds Zen mode, a redesigned 50-level Treasure Hunt, Game Center leaderboards and achievements, and removes lives. Totix plays offline, and every tile color has its own shape for color-blind players.

## How it plays

- Place the next tile on an empty slot of the 4×4 board, or stack it on a tile with the same number.
- Three or four in a line clears for points. Lines run across rows, columns and diagonals, including the short three-tile diagonals.
- Three combo types. Points for a Triple / Quad with the starting deck:
  - Color (one shared color): 100 / 250
  - Number (one shared number): 150 / 375
  - Sequence (a run up or down, like 3-4-5): 250 / 625
- As more colors and numbers join the deck, every combo pays more. Clearing two lines with one tile adds 200 points; three or more adds 500.
- Chain: each consecutive move that makes a match raises the multiplier: ×1, ×1.5, ×2.5, ×4, ×6, then +1 per move (×7, ×8 and so on). The first move without a match resets it. There is no timer on a chain.
- There is no gravity. Tiles never fall or cascade.
- 10 boosters: Freeze Time, Destroy Tile, Swap Tiles, Wildcard Draw, Bomb, Mini Bomb, Shuffle, Peek+1, Color Bomb and Quad Charge. Up to three per run, each single-use.

## Modes

- **Training Arena:** endless score runs that end when no tile fits. A new number joins the deck every minute and a new color every 7.5 minutes.
- **Treasure Hunt:** 50 hand-built levels, each with a mission and three star targets. Levels 1–5 are timed; from level 6, levels have a fixed move budget. Levels 15, 25, 35 and 45 are 60-second speed rounds. 16 levels use fog (only the tile in hand is visible). Levels 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 are boss levels where boosters are not allowed. Ten mission types include score targets, Quads, Sequences, chains, double combos, stacks and locked tiles.
- **Zen:** no clock and no game over. When the board fills, it clears and play continues until the player finishes the session.
- **Daily Event:** the same starting deal for everyone each day, three tries, and a 60-second clock on every move. The best try counts. Scores earn Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond rewards.
- **Weekly League:** one timed trial a day, Monday to Sunday (UTC). Daily scores add up to a weekly total that earns a Bronze to Diamond reward. Worldwide rankings are on Game Center.
- Daily Event and Weekly League unlock after three finished runs.

## Accessibility

Every tile color has its own shape: red triangle, blue circle, green square, yellow diamond, purple star, orange hexagon (later colors add a heart, pentagon, cross and crescent). The shapes always show as a faint stamp; the Color-blind shapes setting makes them bold. Also: a high-contrast palette tuned for deuteranopia, reduced motion (follows the iOS setting by default), larger text, adjustable drag sensitivity, separate music, sound and vibration controls, and replayable tutorials.

## Business model

Free to play. Optional purchases: coin packs ($2.49–$19.99), booster packs ($0.99–$7.99), a one-time Starter Pack ($1.99) and Totix Premium ($3.99, one-time), which turns off the ads shown between levels. Gems are earned in play and never sold. Prices are in USD; the App Store shows local prices.

Ads: none in a player's first session or first 24 hours, at most one for every three finished runs, never in Training Arena or Zen, and no banner ads. Rewarded videos are optional and only continue a run or double a gem reward.

## Toti

Toti is the Totix mascot: a round, cheerful underwater creature with a gold tuft who cheers on every combo. Six expressions (neutral, excited, awed, triumphant, concerned, sleepy) and five outfits (Trainer, Gem Hunter, Champion, Royal, Pirate), all free to pick.
