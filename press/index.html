The game in 20 seconds

Cozy on the surface, deep underneath

There is no gravity in Totix. Tiles stay exactly where you put them, so every combo is one you set up. A calm board turns into a puzzle about planning two or three moves ahead to keep a chain alive.

Short description

Totix: Number Match Puzzle is a free puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Players drag numbered tiles onto a 4×4 board and complete lines of three (a Triple) or four (a Quad) that share a color, share a number, or count in a run such as 3-4-5. Tiles never fall, so every combo is planned, and matching on consecutive moves builds a chain multiplier. Version 1.1 adds Zen mode, a redesigned 50-level Treasure Hunt, Game Center leaderboards and achievements, and removes lives. Totix plays offline, and every tile color has its own shape for color-blind players.

How it plays

Three ways to match

Place the next tile on an empty slot, or stack it on a tile with the same number. Complete three or four in a line across a row, a column or a diagonal and the line clears for points. A Quad pays far more than a Triple.

The chain

Match on back-to-back moves

Every move that makes a match pushes the multiplier one step up. The first move without a match resets it. There is no timer on a chain, so the skill is in planning which line to finish next.

Modes

Five ways to play

Training Arena, Treasure Hunt and Zen are open from the start. Daily Event and Weekly League unlock after three finished runs. Everything plays offline.

Endless

Training Arena

Score-chasing runs that end when no tile fits. A new number joins the deck every minute and a new color every 7.5 minutes.

Campaign

Treasure Hunt

50 levels, each with a mission and three star targets. Most give a fixed number of moves; four are 60-second speed rounds.

Relax

Zen

No clock and no game over. A full board clears itself and the session goes on until you finish it.

Daily

Daily Event

Everyone gets the same starting deal each day, with three tries and a 60-second clock on every move. The best try counts.

Weekly

Weekly League

One timed trial a day, Monday to Sunday. Daily scores add up to a weekly total that earns a Bronze to Diamond reward.

Treasure Hunt, level by level

Ten mission types run through World 1: score targets, Quads, Sequences, chains, double combos, stacks, locked tiles and more. The deck grows from 5 colors and numbers 1 to 10 at the start to 8 colors and numbers up to 24 by the end.

Accessibility

Every color has a shape

Each tile color carries its own symbol, so a match never depends on telling colors apart. The shapes are always there as a faint stamp; the Color-blind shapes setting makes them bold.

  • High contrast: a palette tuned for deuteranopia.
  • Reduce motion: calmer celebrations; follows the iOS setting by default.
  • Larger text and an adjustable drag sensitivity.
  • Separate controls for music, sound effects and vibration, and tutorials can be replayed any time.

Background

Why Totix exists

Totix started from one design question: what if a match puzzle had no gravity? Take away falling tiles and every clear becomes something you set up on purpose. A cozy little board quietly turns into a game of planning and chains: friendly enough to enjoy in one tap, deep enough to chase a high score for weeks.

Meet Toti, a round, cheerful underwater creature with a gold tuft who cheers on every combo. Toti has six expressions and five outfits (Trainer, Gem Hunter, Champion, Royal and Pirate), all free to pick.

Business model

Free to play. Coin packs ($2.49 to $19.99), booster packs ($0.99 to $7.99) and a one-time Starter Pack ($1.99) are optional. Gems are earned in play and never sold. Totix Premium ($3.99, one-time) turns off the ads shown between levels. Prices are in USD; the App Store shows local prices.

Ads stay out of the way: none in a player’s first session or first 24 hours, at most one for every three finished runs, never in Training Arena or Zen, and no banners. Watching a video is always optional and only ever continues a run or doubles a gem reward.
