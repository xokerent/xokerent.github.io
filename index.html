Cozy on the surface, deep underneath

Tiles never fall in Totix. They stay exactly where you put them, so every combo is one you set up, and a calm board becomes a puzzle about keeping your chain alive.

  • Chain multipliers: match on back-to-back moves and climb ×1.5, ×2.5, ×4, ×6 and beyond.
  • Treasure Hunt: 50 levels with missions, fog levels, speed rounds and five bosses.
  • Zen mode: no clock and no game over.
  • No more lives: retry a level straight away, as often as you like.
  • Game Center: worldwide leaderboards and 10 achievements.
  • Color-blind shapes: every tile color has its own symbol.
How it plays

Three ways to match

Place each tile on an empty slot, or stack it on a tile with the same number. Three or four in a row, column or diagonal clears for points.
